Please read these terms carefully before using our services.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this website, booking an appointment, or authorizing vehicle repairs with AutoCare, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree to these terms, please do not use our services.

2. Estimates and Authorizations

Initial Estimates: All written and verbal estimates provided are approximate and based on standard industry labor times and current part costs. Estimates are subject to change upon physical tear-down and inspection of the vehicle.

Authorizations: No repair work will be performed without your express verbal or written authorization. We reserve the right to charge diagnostic or tear-down fees even if you decline subsequent repairs.

3. Payment Terms & Mechanics Lien

Payment in full is due immediately upon completion of authorized services unless a prior corporate account agreement is in place. We accept major credit cards, cash, and authorized digital payments.

Mechanics Lien: In accordance with state law, AutoCare retains an express mechanics lien on your vehicle to secure the amount of repairs thereto. Vehicles will not be released until full payment clears.

4. Limited Warranty on Repairs

We stand behind our work. AutoCare provides a standard 12-month or 12,000-mile warranty (whichever comes first) on parts and labor for all standard repairs.

Exclusions: This warranty strictly excludes customer-supplied parts, used parts, high-performance modifications, and damage caused by subsequent accidents, misuse, off-roading, or lack of routine maintenance.

5. Limitation of Liability

AutoCare is not responsible for loss or damage to your vehicle or articles left in the vehicle in case of fire, theft, accident, or any other cause beyond our control. Please remove all valuables from your vehicle prior to service.

6. Vehicle Abandonment

Vehicles left on our property for more than 48 hours after you have been notified of repair completion will be subject to a storage fee of $45 per day. Vehicles left unpaid for over 30 days will be considered abandoned and may be sold at public auction to satisfy the lien.

7. Refund Policy

Labor charges are strictly non-refundable once work has been completed. Parts may be eligible for a return or replacement only if they fail under the manufacturer's warranty period. Special order parts are non-refundable and require a 50% upfront deposit.

8. Loaner Car Agreement

If provided a loaner vehicle, the driver must be at least 21 years of age, possess a valid driver's license, and carry active comprehensive auto insurance. The customer is strictly responsible for all fuel used, tolls, parking violations, and any physical damage sustained to the loaner vehicle while in their possession.

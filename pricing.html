Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize a service package?

Absolutely. While our standard packages cover the majority of needs, we can tailor a specific maintenance plan based on your vehicle's make, model, and mileage.

Do you use OEM parts?

Yes, we default to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts for all replacements to ensure maximum reliability and preserve your warranty. Aftermarket options are available upon request.

Are there any hidden disposal fees?

No. Our pricing is completely transparent. The price you see includes all labor, parts, and environmental disposal fees for fluids and old parts.

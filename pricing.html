Frequently Asked Questions

Can I customize a service package?
Absolutely. While our standard packages cover the majority of needs, we can tailor a specific maintenance plan based on your vehicle's make, model, and mileage.
Do you use OEM parts?
Yes, we default to Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts for all replacements to ensure maximum reliability and preserve your warranty. Aftermarket options are available upon request.
Is there a warranty on your repairs?
Yes, all our services and repairs come with a standard 12-month / 12,000-mile warranty covering both parts and labor, ensuring your complete peace of mind.
Are there any hidden fees?
Never. We believe in radical transparency. You will receive a detailed estimate before any work begins, and we will never proceed with additional repairs without your explicit approval.

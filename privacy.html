Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, or could reasonably be linked to you or your vehicle:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, physical address, email address, phone number.
  • Vehicle Information: VIN, license plate, make, model, year, service history, and diagnostic codes.
  • Financial Information: Payment card details (processed securely via encrypted third-party gateways).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly utilized for the following operational purposes:

  • Providing estimates, diagnosing vehicle issues, and performing requested automotive repairs.
  • Communicating with you regarding appointment scheduling, repair statuses, and post-service follow-ups.
  • Fulfilling warranty claims and tracking vehicle service intervals.
  • Processing payments and preventing fraudulent transactions.

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third-party marketers. We may share your data with trusted service providers (such as parts suppliers needing a VIN for compatibility, or payment processors) strictly for the purpose of fulfilling your service request. We may also disclose data when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We implement robust, industry-standard physical and digital security measures to protect your personal and vehicle data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Customer portal passwords are cryptographically hashed, and all payment data is processed via PCI-DSS compliant gateways.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate data, or request the deletion of your data (subject to legally mandated record-keeping requirements for vehicle repairs and financial transactions). To exercise these rights, please contact us directly.

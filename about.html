Our History

What started as a modest two-bay garage in 2010 has rapidly expanded into a premier, multi-location automotive repair franchise. We recognized early on that the industry needed a shift towards complete transparency and digital integration.

Today, AutoCare serves thousands of vehicles annually, utilizing cutting-edge diagnostic equipment to ensure precision repairs every single time.
